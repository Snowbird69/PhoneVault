Android

Android phones back up over USB debugging: texts, picture messages, Google Messages chats, call history, contacts, photos, videos and voice recordings.

Android phone? Turn on USB debugging (one time)
  1. Open Settings › About phone and tap Build number 7 times until it says you're a developer. (Samsung: Settings › About phone › Software information. Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO: tap OS version / MIUI version.)
  2. Go back to Settings › System › Developer options (Samsung: Settings › Developer options) and turn on USB debugging.
  3. Plug the phone in, unlock it, and tap Allow on "Allow USB debugging?" (tick Always allow from this computer).
  4. If the phone asks what the USB connection is for, choose File transfer.

Windows: if the phone still doesn't show, install your brand's USB driver (Samsung: "Samsung Android USB Driver"; Pixel: "Google USB Driver").

Import: for messages Android hides from other apps (Samsung chat photos, end-to-end encrypted RCS), back up with the free SMS Backup & Restore app and import its .xml files here. Export: makes .xml files that SMS Backup & Restore puts onto any Android phone, from an iPhone or Android archive.

Restore to phone

Copies archived messages, calls and photos onto the plugged-in Android phone. Nothing on the phone is deleted or replaced. The source can be this phone's archive or another phone's, including an iPhone.

  1. Messages and calls are copied as SMS Backup & Restore files into the phone's Download/PhoneVault folder. Then, on the phone, install the free SMS Backup & Restore app, choose Restore and pick those files. It asks to be the default SMS app while it restores; switch back afterwards.
  2. Photos and videos go into DCIM/PhoneVault. They show in the Gallery/Photos app once the phone has scanned them (restart the phone if they don't appear).

2. What to copy

Puts a saved backup back onto the plugged-in iPhone: messages, contacts, settings, call history and app data. It replaces what's on the phone now. Photos that are already copied stay in the archive either way.

  1. Before you start: on the iPhone, Settings > your name > Find My > Find My iPhone > Off. iOS refuses a restore while it's on.
  2. PhoneVault backs up the phone first and keeps that copy as a dated snapshot, so you can restore back to today.
  3. The backup you pick is never changed. The restore reads a temporary working copy.
  4. The phone restarts when it's done, then re-downloads apps over Wi-Fi.

Phone view

Opens this archive in your iPhone's browser over home Wi-Fi. Read old threads, play voice notes, and save photos, audio or a chat as PDF onto the phone.

  1. Press Start phone view.
  2. Scan the code with the iPhone camera and tap the link.
  3. The link is private and read-only, and switches itself off after 30 minutes.

On the phone: tap Save under a picture or recording, or open a chat and tap Save as PDF, then choose Save to Files.
